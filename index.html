Our Story

Forged in
El Paso

Family-owned and operated for over 30 years, we've built everything from custom driveway gates to heavy-duty commercial structures across the Borderland.

Sal's Ironworks started with one welder, one vision, and a relentless commitment to quality. Three decades later, that same commitment drives every weld we make. We don't cut corners — we cut steel.

From residential gates and pool railings to commercial pergolas and industrial fabrication, we take on projects that require real craftsmanship. Our work speaks louder than any slogan.

What We Build

Our Services

Every project is custom. Every piece is fabricated in-house. No off-the-shelf solutions — just ironwork built exactly the way you want it.

Residential / Commercial

Gates

Driveway gates, pedestrian entry gates, automated or manual. Custom designs in wrought iron, steel, and mixed materials.

Interior / Exterior

Railings

Stair railings, balcony rails, pool barriers, and handrails. Built to code, designed to last, finished to impress.

Pool / Patio / Deck

Pergolas

Steel-framed pergolas engineered for El Paso's climate. From sleek modern structures to ornate custom designs.

Property & Security

Fencing

Perimeter fencing, pool enclosures, decorative yard fencing. Security and curb appeal in one custom solution.

Industrial / Commercial

Structural Steel

Stairs, platforms, frames, and custom structural elements for commercial and industrial applications.

One-of-a-Kind

Custom Fabrication

Artistic panels, decorative elements, commercial signage frames, and one-of-a-kind ironwork — if you can dream it, we can weld it.
