Our Story

Family-Owned.
El Paso Made.

Sal's Ironworks and Fabrication was built on a simple belief: if it carries your name, it better be built right. From day one, we've put craftsmanship above all else — measuring twice, welding once, and standing behind every piece we put into the world.

We're rooted here in El Paso. This is our home, these are our neighbors, and every job we take — whether it's a single stair railing or a full commercial enclosure — gets the same pride and precision we'd put into our own property.

From residential homeowners wanting something beautiful to industrial clients needing something bulletproof, we bring the same standards to every weld and every job site.
